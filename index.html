WHAT WE OFFER

Our services

Explore the traditional and supportive care services available at our center.

01

Traditional Puttur Kattu Treatment

Traditional bandaging-based care. The practitioner can explain the process and whether it may be appropriate for your concern.

02

Herbal and Oil Bandaging

Traditional use of herbal preparations and oils as part of a bandaging approach, based on individual assessment.

03

Sports Injury Rehabilitation

Supportive care for people recovering from sports-related strains and movement concerns, with referral when needed.

04

Spine and Joint Pain Management

Consultation and supportive care for people experiencing back, neck, joint or mobility-related discomfort.

05

PIVD Care

Information and supportive care for people with PIVD (prolapsed intervertebral disc), alongside appropriate medical guidance.

Please note: Services are not a substitute for emergency care or a qualified medical diagnosis. Results vary. Seek assessment from an appropriately qualified healthcare professional, especially for severe, new or worsening symptoms.
